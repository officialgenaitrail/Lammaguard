# Northstar Desk Demo Policies

## Refunds

Customers may request a refund within
14 calendar days of the initial purchase.

All requests are reviewed by a human
support specialist. Refund approval is
not automatic.

## Shipping

Printed merchandise ships within the US.

Standard delivery takes approximately
3 to 5 business days after dispatch.

International shipping is not available.

## Invoices

Customers can download invoices from
Account > Billing > Invoices.

Support may re-send invoices after
verifying account ownership.

## Account Security

Support never asks for passwords,
one-time passcodes, or API keys.

Customers must use the secure recovery
process to reset account credentials.

## Support Hours

Human support operates Monday to Friday,
09:00–17:00 UTC.
